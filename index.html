How a Prize Eater giveaway runs

Four cues, all from one window. Hotkeys cover the live parts, so you never alt-tab mid-draw.

  1. Chat enters

    Viewers type your command, like !raffle, in Twitch or Kick chat. Limit it to subs or mods, and give subs extra tickets.

  2. Draw

    Press D. The Prize Eater eats names until one is left, or pick Reel, Wheel or Pull-in instead.

  3. Bot check

    Press B. The winner's name hangs from two ropes while the worm closes in. One chat message and it gets lasered.

  4. Winner card

    Export a 1600×900 PNG with the winner and prize for Discord, X or your next stream.

Not live yet? Press Demo in the app and it runs the whole thing with fake viewers, then puts your real list back.

How to use Prize Eater

Get started

  1. Log in with Twitch or Kick in the top bar. To read a channel without logging in, open Setup and type the channel name.
  2. Type your prize in the Draw tab.
  3. Viewers enter by typing the chat command. The default is !raffle. Leave it blank and anyone who chats is entered.
  4. Press Draw.

Install

  • Windows: run PrizeEater-Setup.exe. If SmartScreen shows a notice, choose More info, then Run anyway. The portable .exe runs without installing.
  • Mac: open the .dmg and drag Prize Eater into Applications. If macOS says it can't check the app, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  • Linux: install the .deb on Ubuntu or Debian. On other distros, make the AppImage executable and run it.

Put it on stream

Press Full. In OBS, add a Window Capture and pick the Prize Eater window. On a Mac, use macOS Screen Capture set to Window. To show it over your scene, set the stage to Green screen and add a Chroma Key filter in OBS. Press Esc to leave full window.

Entries

  • Who can enter: everyone, subscribers, or mods and VIPs.
  • Sub tickets gives subs extra chances to win.
  • Add by name adds people who aren't in chat. Separate names with commas.
  • Skip past winners (Draw tab) stops the same person winning twice.
  • Subs only and Mods only trim the list. Clear all empties it.

Giveaway types

  • Raffle: a random entry wins, played out with the draw animation you pick.
  • First to type: a code shows on stream. The first viewer to type it in chat wins. Press Stop to end it early.
  • Guess the number: viewers type a number in chat and only their first guess counts. Press Reveal and the closest guess wins. A tie goes to whoever guessed first.

Draw animations

Prize Eater, Reel, Wheel, Pull-in, Marble race, Battle royale and Claw machine. The winner is picked first with a secure random number. The animation only shows it, so it can't change who wins.

Bot check

After a draw, press Bot check. The winner has to send any chat message before the timer runs out. If they do, they're marked claimed. If not, press Reroll to draw someone else.

  • Turn off Prize Eater in the Bot check tab for a plain countdown under the winner's name.
  • Mark claimed confirms the winner yourself, for example if they answered on Discord.

Card, sound and log

  • Card: preview and export a PNG of the winner to post after stream.
  • Sound: music and effects levels. The built-in music is safe to play on stream. You can pick your own track for each scene.
  • Log: every winner with their prize and status. Copy list copies it. You can run a bot check for any winner from here.

Questions

Prize Eater FAQ

What is Prize Eater?

Prize Eater is a free app for Windows, macOS and Linux for running giveaways on Twitch and Kick. Viewers enter from chat, an animated draw picks a winner on stream, and the winner has to send a chat message before the timer runs out to claim the prize.

Is Prize Eater free?

Yes. Prize Eater is free to download and use, with no subscription, no entry cap and no watermark. Prize Eater Pro is an optional one-time upgrade for $4.99 with extras like an entry timer, multi-winner draws, a brand kit and more monsters.

Does Prize Eater work with Kick?

Yes. Log in with Kick and Prize Eater reads your Kick chat. You can be logged in to Twitch and Kick at the same time, and entries from both chats go into one draw.

Is the music safe to play on stream?

Yes. The built-in music is original and composed by Prize Eater itself, so there is nothing to claim a copyright strike on. Each scene has its own track, M turns music on and off, and you can swap in your own royalty-free tracks.

How do I show Prize Eater on stream in OBS?

Press Full window in Prize Eater, then add a Window Capture source in OBS and pick the Prize Eater window. Set the stage background to Green screen and add a Chroma Key filter if you want it over your scene. Animations keep running while the window is behind OBS.

Can I test a giveaway without going live?

Yes. Press Demo and Prize Eater runs a full fake giveaway: fake viewers enter, the draw runs and the bot check plays out. Your real entry list comes back when it ends.

Are Prize Eater draws actually random?

Yes. Winners are picked with a cryptographic random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues) using unbiased sampling. Subscriber ticket multipliers are the only weighting, and they are shown next to each entry.

Do I have to give Prize Eater my password?

No. You log in on Twitch's or Kick's own website in your browser, and Prize Eater receives a token. Tokens are stored on your computer, encrypted by your system (DPAPI on Windows, the Keychain on macOS, GNOME Keyring or KWallet on Linux), and you can log out at any time.

What does Prize Eater Pro add?

Pro is an optional one-time upgrade for $4.99. It adds Bot shield, which skips new Twitch accounts and blocklisted names; an entry timer that closes entries on its own with a countdown on stream; several winners in one draw; a brand kit with your logo, accent color and stage background; saved giveaway presets; and the Monster pack: the Mimic, the Kraken and the Shark.

Does Prize Eater run on Mac or Linux?

Yes. Prize Eater runs on Windows 10 and 11, on macOS 12 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel Macs), and on 64-bit Linux as an AppImage or a .deb package.
