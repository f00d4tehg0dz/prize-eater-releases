# Prize Eater

**Giveaways your chat has to fight for.** Prize Eater is a free Windows app for running Twitch and Kick giveaways on stream. Viewers enter from chat, an animated draw picks the winner, and the winner has to chat before the worm eats their name.

**Website:** https://prizeeater.app

**Windows SmartScreen:** on first launch Windows may say it protected your PC, because the app is new and not yet widely downloaded. Choose **More info → Run anyway**.

## Features

- Viewers enter from **Twitch and Kick** chat at the same time, with a custom command like `!raffle`.
- Subscriber-only or mods-and-VIPs-only giveaways, and extra tickets for subs.
- Draw animations: **Prize Eater**, Reel, Wheel and Pull-in, picked with a cryptographic random number generator.
- **Bot check:** the winner's name is tied up while the worm closes in. One chat message fires a laser and saves them.
- Cartoon or scary worm, built-in music that's safe to stream, or your own tracks.
- Full-window stage and green screen for OBS, hotkeys, and a winner card PNG.
- Log in with Twitch or Kick in your browser. Prize Eater never sees your password.

**Prize Eater Pro** is an optional one-time upgrade that adds Bot shield (skip new Twitch accounts and blocklisted names) and your own logo on the stage and winner card.
